## Hook
You can generate a stunning image in 30 seconds. Turning it into something a stranger will pay for — a poster, a tee, a sticker — used to mean suppliers, mockups, pricing spreadsheets and a warehouse. Not anymore.

## What "selling AI art" actually requires
To earn from AI art you need four things most tools don't give you:
1. **A product.** The art has to become a physical object people buy.
2. **Manufacturing.** Someone has to print, press or 3D-print it on demand.
3. **A storefront.** Buyers need a place to find and pay.
4. **Fulfilment.** It has to ship, track and arrive.

Desmake collapses all four into one publish action.

## The old way vs the Desmake way
- **Old:** generate → download → open a POD dashboard → create a product per SKU → upload mockups → write copy → set price → repeat for every variant.
- **Desmake:** generate or upload one design → it is auto-adapted across six manufacturing methods (apparel, posters, stickers, phone cases, business cards, 3D prints) → mockups, copy and SEO are written for you → you set your royalty (10–50%) → it lists.

## Step-by-step: publish your first design
1. Open Studio and generate or upload artwork.

3. Set your royalty per product.
4. Publish. The listing goes live with a cost breakdown buyers can see.

## Pricing without the spreadsheet
Every Desmake listing shows three numbers: manufacturing cost, your margin, retail price. You never price blind. Start at a $0 margin while you learn the catalogue; raise it whenever you like.

## Getting your first sale
- Lead with your best single image, not a 50-design dump.
- Pick a coherent style (abstract, typography, botanical) so your storefront reads as a brand.
- Share the direct listing link in communities where that style lives.
