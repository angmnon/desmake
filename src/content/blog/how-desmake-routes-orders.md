## The problem with one factory
A single print shop means one price, one lead time, one shipping zone. For a global marketplace that's slow and expensive.

## Desmake's approach
Every order is matched to the manufacturing node with the **best cost-to-door**:
- The buyer's location sets the shipping target.
- Each adapter quotes cost + lead time for the product and print method.
- Desmake picks the node that minimises total delivered cost while meeting quality bars.

## Why creators don't see this
You set a royalty; Desmake handles the logistics. The cost breakdown on every listing shows manufacturing cost and your margin — transparent, not hidden.

## What buyers get
- Shorter delivery (made closer to home).
- Lower shipping cost (no cross-continent haul).
- Same product, wherever they are.
