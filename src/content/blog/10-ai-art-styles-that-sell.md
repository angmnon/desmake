Based on what lists and sells on Desmake, these styles consistently perform:

1. **Abstract gradients** — safe, modern, fits any room or tee.
2. **Bold typography quotes** — evergreen; pair a strong line with clean type.
3. **Geometric minimalism** — sharp, desk-friendly, great as stickers.
4. **Botanical illustration** — strong in home decor and phone cases.
5. **Retro 80s/90s** — nostalgia sells; neon and grainy textures win.
6. **Line art** — single-weight drawings read well small (cases, stickers).
7. **3D rendered objects** — novelty appeal; also printable as real objects.
8. **Dupes of "aesthetic" moods** — coquette, cottagecore, dark academia.
9. **Map & cityscape line work** — location pride sells locally.
10. **Mascots & characters** — if you own the IP, characters build a brand.

## How to use this list
- Pick one or two styles and go deep — a coherent storefront outsells a random pile.
- Enable the SKUs that fit the style (typography → tees + posters; botanical → posters + cases).
- Let Desmake write the copy; you focus on the art.
