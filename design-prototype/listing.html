About this design

Signal Decay explores the tension between digital precision and organic entropy. The composition uses a controlled gradient mesh overlaid with sharp geometric interruptions — each element placed to feel both intentional and slightly off-kilter.

Available as a museum-quality giclée poster, heavyweight DTG-printed T-shirt, or die-cut vinyl sticker pack. Every product is manufactured on demand from the same source file, ensuring colour fidelity across all formats.
