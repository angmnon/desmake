Why D2M

Designers make files.
Desmake makes them products.

The gap between a beautiful file and a shippable object is tooling, colour science, packaging and logistics. The D2M engine closes it in one pass — so publishing a product costs the same as posting an image.

01
Adapters, not templates

Each adapter carries substrate, ink, bleed, colour profile and tolerance data. Your file is re-rendered per method — never stretched onto a stock photo.

02
Auto-dressed listings

Title, copy, tags and price bands are drafted from the artwork itself plus live comparables, then handed to you for a 30-second edit.

03
Factory routing

Orders go to the nearest certified partner with capacity — 41 facilities across 14 countries, scored on quality, lead time and carbon.

04
Agent readable

Every published product is exposed through MCP and REST, so agents can search, quote and order it without a human in the loop.
